**Ideas I got**:

Once you defeat the worm in the tutorial area, you walk forward a bit and the camera pans out to a series of platforms with a floating yellow orb at the top (singular nectarino). Sherry and Charlotte tell you to go get the nectar, and this is kind of the first bit of solo platforming the player does. The player makes their way up to the nectar and stops next to the nectar, where the cherry girls explain the importance of the nectar, and say that you cannot eat the nectar, as a joke. You collect the nectar and they say that the next part of the level is on you, and you should reach the outpost soon. You make your way through the level, doing simple platforming, defeating worms, and getting 2 more nectars. Then, you reach the outpost and get introduced to all of the fruit characters. Then, the level is beat.
