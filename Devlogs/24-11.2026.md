# Winter Sprites - November 24, 2026
**Date: 24.11.2026**

**Time spent: ~5 hours | Mood: dizzy**

**What I did today**: 
- I worked on a lot of the sprites for the game. I made a sprite for Appy, a sprite for Sherry, nectar, and the mud. 
- I made a sprite for Appy with and without the bite, and it's more of a placeholder sprite since he does not have a walking animation in it, or really turn in any direction. 
- I made a sprite for Sherry, and I'll have to fix its position possibly tomorrow. I really like the sprite I did for her, it's only one of the sprites I plan to do for her, and I'm just using it as a general placeholder. I wanted to make her look really cartoonishly shocked, with white pupil-less eyes, and her breaking the borders of the radio box to hang her arms out. I'm planning a similar shocked sprite for Charlotte, but with her grabbing her head instead. I want Sherry and Charlotte to look similar since they're twins, but for all of their poses to be different. Sherry will also be dark red and Charlotte will be a pinkish red, to contrast with Appy and each other. 
- I made a nectar sprite, and I drew my own circle to have that really scratchy look I'm going for. Not much to say about it, tried to use hue-shifting with the yellow and the red to make it look nice. 
- I made the sprite for a mud ball, which I may have to edit because it might be too detailed for such a small ball of mud.
- I imported all of the sprites and dealt with the issues that arose. For example, changing the position and size of Appy caused the mud launcher to just not fire, since the muzzle controls where the mud comes out of and the mud launcher is the child of the muzzle, so the muzzle would spawn inside the ground causing the mud balls to just disappear immediately. I adjusted all of the tools to prevent it, though the mud balls may still not be perfectly aligned with the tools so I'll have to fix that. I also had to adjust the how the sprites fit into the colliders, cause they were above the colliders when I first imported them. Changed Appy's collider from the circle to the capsule cause it fit his shape more. 
- I also fixed the second platform, since it was extended further than it should have been, even though it was hidden behind the next one, which caused Appy to just be able to walk on air.

**Ideas I got**:
Sherry and Charlotte are twins and were connected with one stem (like usual depictions of cherries), however the worms chewed through their stem and that is their injury. All of the fruits have injuries, like Appy's worm bite, and progressing through the story would be like healing them, and you'd be able to unlock more characters as you go along. All characters will probably have their own abilities. I'm also planning to have a life system (not developed yet) where whenever Appy loses a heart, he looks progressively more beat up. It will probably fit into the scribbly, grimy feel I'm going for. I'm planning for the level select being a map you receive at the outpost, and saving and settings being a walkie talkie where a random character kind of walks you through the options and you select what you want to do, like in the older Animal Crossing games. I'll try to integrate all of the game choices into the world itself.
